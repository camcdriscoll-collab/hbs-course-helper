# CLASS-SPECIFIC NOTES

This session covers works by four distinct authors. Structure the podcast to give each author dedicated in-depth coverage:

For each author (approximately 10–12 minutes each):
- Their central thesis and the historical moment in which they wrote
- Key mechanisms or frameworks they introduce
- How their argument applies to contemporary capitalism-state dynamics
- What to emphasize in class discussion — the strongest version of their argument and the most likely counterarguments

**Duration: approximately 45–50 minutes total.** Be substantive and specific — name each author, the actual text, and specific concepts. Do not rush through any author to keep time — equal depth matters more than a strict clock.
